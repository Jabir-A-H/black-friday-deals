### 4. Technical Deep Dive: Decision-Maker Features

1. **External GPU (eGPU) Compatibility via Thunderbolt 4 / USB4 (40Gbps):**
   * Laptops equipped with **Thunderbolt 4, Thunderbolt 3, or USB4 (40Gbps)** provide 4 PCIe Gen 3/4 lanes directly to the processor.
   * **Why it matters:** You can dock a lightweight laptop (like a ThinkPad X1 Yoga, Latitude 7320/7330, or EliteBook 1040) to an external GPU enclosure (such as a Razer Core X with a desktop RTX 4070/4080) via a single cable. You get a 1.2 kg ultraportable on the go, and a desktop-class AI/rendering battlestation at home!
   * Models without TB4/USB4 (standard USB-C 3.2 5Gbps/10Gbps) **cannot** drive external GPUs.

2. **RAM Architecture: Soldered LPDDR vs. Un-Soldered SODIMM Slots:**
   * **Soldered LPDDR4x / LPDDR5x:** Fast (up to 6400–7467 MT/s) and energy-efficient, but permanently locked to the motherboard. **Never buy an 8GB soldered laptop in 2026** — it will be bottlenecked by Windows 11 and browser tabs within months. Look for ≥16GB or 32GB pre-installed.
   * **Un-Soldered SODIMM Slots:** Upgrades can be performed at home with a $25–$45 DDR4/DDR5 stick. Models like the **Latitude 5420/5430/5530, EliteBook 840/845, ProBook 445/x360 435, ThinkBook 14s Yoga, ThinkPad P14s, Precision 3581, Victus 15, and ThinkPad T14 Gen 2/3** feature upgradeable memory up to 40GB–64GB total.

3. **Storage Configuration & Dual Drive Slots:**
   * **Single M.2 Slots:** Most ultrabooks (X1 Carbon, Latitude 7320/7330, EliteBook 830) have 1× M.2 2280 slot (must clone/swap drive to upgrade).
   * **Dual Drive Slots:** The **ThinkBook 14s Yoga** (2× M.2 slots), **Dell Precision 3581** (2× M.2 slots), **Acer Nitro V 15** (2× M.2 slots), **ASUS TUF A15** (2× M.2 slots), and **MSI GF63** (1× M.2 + 1× 2.5" SATA HDD/SSD bay) allow you to add a secondary 1TB/2TB drive without touching your OS drive!

4. **Active Stylus Integration: Garaged Rechargeable vs. External Loose Pens:**
   * **Garaged Pens (The Gold Standard):** Integrated inside the laptop body. Includes **ThinkPad X1 Yoga, ThinkPad X13 Yoga (Gen 2/3), ThinkPad L13 Yoga (Gen 2/3), and ThinkBook 14s Yoga**. The pen slides into an internal silo, supercharges to 80% in 15 seconds, has 4,096 levels of Wacom AES pressure, and is physically impossible to forget at home.
   * **External Pens:** Used by Dell 2-in-1s and HP Envy. Often require AAAA batteries, must be carried loose or attached via weak magnets, and frequently get misplaced.

5. **SD Card Interfaces (Full-Size vs. MicroSD vs. Dongle):**
   * **Full-Size SD Card Reader:** Present on **HP Victus 15, Dell Inspiron 14 2-in-1, Dell Inspiron 15, Lenovo IdeaPad Slim 3i, Lenovo IdeaPad Flex 5, and HP Envy x360**. Crucial for photographers and creators to ingest camera cards directly.
   * **MicroSD Slot:** Standard on almost all commercial business laptops (**ThinkPad T14/L13/X13/P14s, Latitude 5420/5430/7320/7420/7330, Precision 3550/3581, ProBook 445/x360 435, ThinkBook 14s Yoga, Yoga 6**). Great for permanent storage expansion via a 512GB MicroSD card.
   * **No SD Slot:** Flagships like the ThinkPad X1 Yoga and HP EliteBook 1040 sacrifice the SD slot for ultra-slim chassis profiles.
