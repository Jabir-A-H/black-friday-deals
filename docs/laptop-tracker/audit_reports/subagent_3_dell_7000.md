## 💡 Strategic Takeaways for Dell 7000 & 9000 Fleet Buyers

1. **Soldered RAM Alert on 7000 & 9000 Series:**
   Unlike the Dell Latitude 5000 series (which features dual accessible SODIMM slots), **all Latitude 7000 and 9000 series laptops feature soldered LPDDR4x/LPDDR5 memory**. Buyers should prioritize 16GB as the absolute non-negotiable minimum, or target the **Latitude 7430 32GB configuration ($515.98–$559.99)** to permanently avoid RAM constraints for data science, heavy Docker workflows, and multi-tab productivity.
