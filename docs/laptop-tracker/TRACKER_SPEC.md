## 🎯 Purpose & Scope

## 💰 Multi-Tier Budget Brackets & Effective Currency Conversion Model

### 1. Exchange Rate & Landed Cost Breakdown
To accurately represent real purchasing cost in Bangladesh, we track three distinct rate layers:
* **Layer 1: Mid-Market Rate (`1 USD ≈ 124.0 BDT`):** Official Bangladesh Bank & Google/XE mid-market benchmark.
* **Layer 2: Bank Card Settlement Rate (`1 USD ≈ 128.0 BDT`):** Commercial bank card selling rate (~124.50 BDT) + 1.5%–2% cross-border FX markup + 15% VAT on service charges. Used when shipping to a tax-free US address (e.g. Delaware/Oregon) via traveler.
* **Layer 3: Realistic Landed Cost Rate (`1 USD ≈ 135.0 BDT`):** Bank settlement rate + US average state/city sales tax (~6%–8.875%) + transfer/handling buffer. **This is our primary realistic planning benchmark.**

## 📋 Evaluation Rules & Focus Protocols

1. **Strict Floor Benchmark:** Must distinctly match or outperform an **HP EliteBook 840 G8 (Intel Core i5-1135G7, 8GB DDR4, 256GB SSD)**.
2. **Sub-৳55,000 BDT Deep Research Directive:** The agent must exhaustively search and prioritize candidate laptops whose total realistic landed cost stays within **৳50,000 – ৳55,000 BDT** (maximum US cart price: **$370 – $407 USD**).
3. **Touchscreen & 360° Convertible Protocol:**
   * For convertibles with soldered RAM: **16GB RAM minimum is non-negotiable** (8GB soldered convertibles are permanently rejected due to non-upgradeability).
   * Actively monitor for built-in active stylus support (e.g. ThinkPad Pen Pro garaged).
4. **Massive Deal & Outlier Radar (Tier 4):**
   * Even when priced above $550 USD, monitor high-gravity outliers slashed 40%+ (e.g. RTX 4060 laptops at $649, MacBook Air M2 at $649, 32GB RAM 13th Gen corporate flagships) to identify rare opportunities worth stretching the budget for.
5. **RAM Upgradeability Protocol:** 
   * For clamshells: Dual SODIMM slots are mandatory unless pre-installed with ≥16GB RAM.
6. **GPU Evaluation for Local LLMs & Compute:**
   * RTX 4060 (8GB VRAM, ≥75W TGP) = Gold Standard (Runs full 8B LLMs unquantized).
   * RTX 4050 / 3050 (6GB VRAM) = Silver Standard (Runs quantized 8B Q4_K_M).
   * Integrated Iris Xe / Vega 7 = Bronze Standard (Requires 16GB–32GB system RAM).
