## 🏷️ Retailer-Specific Buying Strategies

### 1. Best Buy
* **Price Match Guarantee:** Best Buy offers the **Holiday Price Match Guarantee** on items purchased during Early Black Friday. If Best Buy lowers the price before November 28, you can request a price adjustment refund for the difference.
* **Return Window:** All holiday purchases have an extended return window through **January 15, 2027**.

### 2. Dell Refurbished (DFS)
* **Instant Coupon Codes:** DFS uses rotating coupon codes (e.g. `HOTDEALS`, `DELL50`) that slash 40% to 50% off Grade A enterprise stock.
* **Stock Exhaustion:** DFS stock is tracked by unique 6-digit SKUs (e.g. `000028`). Quantities are strictly limited (typically 5 to 15 units per SKU). Once exhausted, they are not restocked until the next off-lease cycle.

### 3. Walmart & Target
* **Walmart Event Cadence:** Event 1 (Nov 12–15), Event 2 (Nov 19–22), and Peak Black Friday (Nov 25–29). Paid Walmart+ members get a 24-hour early access window.
* **Target Circle Drops:** Target runs 3-day flash events with daily tech drops exclusively for Target Circle members.

### 4. Woot (An Amazon Company)
* **Microsoft Authorized Refurbished (MAR):** Woot lists pristine off-lease enterprise laptops (Latitude 7330, 5420, ThinkPad X13) backed by 90-day to 1-year warranties. Prices are often 20% lower than eBay, but stock typically sells out within 48 to 72 hours.
